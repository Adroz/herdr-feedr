# herdr-feedr

A markdown-backed to-do feed shown as a sidebar in herdr, shared between a human and the agents that pull work from it.

## Language

**Today**:
The human's current focus: at most three uncompleted items, flagged in the feed and shown together at the top of the sidebar. Leaves no trace once an item is done.
_Avoid_: Priority, starred, pinned, focus list
